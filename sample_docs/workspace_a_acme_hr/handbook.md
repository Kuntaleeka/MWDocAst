# Acme Employee Handbook

## Offsite
The Q3 offsite codename is BLUE HERON. It takes place in Lisbon from 14 to 16 October.
Travel is booked by the People team; send your passport name to people@acme.example by 1 October.

## Leave
Full-time staff get 25 days of annual leave plus public holidays.
Unused leave (up to 5 days) carries over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.
Parental leave is 20 weeks at full pay for all parents.

## Expenses
Submit expenses within 30 days through the Ledgerly app.
Meals are reimbursed up to 40 EUR per day while travelling. Alcohol is not reimbursed.
Purchases over 500 EUR need approval from your manager before you buy.

## Remote work
Staff may work remotely up to 3 days a week. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 CET.
The home-office stipend is 300 EUR per year, claimed through Ledgerly.
