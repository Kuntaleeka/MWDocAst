# Acme Benefits Summary 2026

## Health
Private health insurance is provided by Meridian Health and covers partners and children.
Dental cover is included up to 1,000 EUR per year.

## Learning
Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 EUR per year for courses, books and conferences.
Requests go to your manager and are approved within 5 working days.

## Wellbeing
Acme pays up to 50 EUR per month towards a gym or sports membership.
Everyone gets two wellbeing days per year, on top of annual leave.
